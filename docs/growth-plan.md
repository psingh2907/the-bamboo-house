# The Bamboo House — growth plan (visitors & engagement)

Where we start (27 Sep 2026): 4.7★ from 49 Google reviews, Google listing
**unclaimed**, Instagram @the_bamboohouse_resort (501 followers, 71 posts),
new website in preview. Two audiences: **local families** (Hindi-first, come
back often) and **travellers** heading to Panna Tiger Reserve / Khajuraho
(one visit, found on Google Maps, share on Instagram).

The website is one piece. Most visits will start on **Google Maps**, from a
**friend's recommendation**, or on **Instagram**, so the plan works on all three.

---

## 1. Be found — Google Maps first (highest impact, free)

| Action | Why | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Claim the Google Business Profile** | Unlocks everything below; today anyone can edit it | Owner (15 min + postcard/video verification) |
| Fix name + Hindi name, add website, all hours, menu, price range, attributes (family-friendly, parking, veg) | Complete profiles rank higher in "restaurants near me" | Owner, we help |
| **Upload 5 photos every week** (food, interior at dusk, people) | Photo count and freshness are ranking signals; travellers choose by photos | Staff |
| **One Google Post a week** (thali of the week, festival special) | Shows the listing is alive | Owner / us |
| Reply to **every** review within 48 h (English or Hindi) | Replies lift rating trust and ranking | Owner |
| Seed Q&A: parking? veg? group bookings? timings? | Answers travellers' questions before they call | Us |

## 2. Reviews — from 49 to 200 in six months

- **Table QR card** ("Enjoyed your meal? 30 seconds on Google helps us a lot")
  → direct review link (available once the listing is claimed).
- Staff ask **at the bill**, not before; a printed line on the bill with the QR.
- After a group booking, a WhatsApp thank-you with the review link.
- ⚠️ Never offer discounts *for* reviews — Google removes incentivised reviews.

## 3. Travellers — catch the tiger-reserve traffic

- **Safari packed breakfast / lunch boxes.** Morning safaris start early and
  tourists need food; a pre-order box on WhatsApp is a new product. *(Only if
  the route makes sense — owner to confirm distances first.)*
- **Driver & guide partnerships.** Safari gypsy drivers and hotel desks decide
  where tourists stop. Offer drivers a free meal with every group they bring.
- **Listings:** Tripadvisor, Justdial, Zomato (dine-in), and ask Madhya Pradesh
  Tourism to list the restaurant on its Panna page (it lists only two today).
- **"Stop here" signage** on the approach road with the Google rating (4.7★).
- The website's Panna attractions section + future travel guides (Journal)
  capture "where to eat near Panna Tiger Reserve" searches.

## 4. Locals — make it the family's regular place

- **Weekly rhythm:** Sunday family thali, a weekday lunch thali, festival
  thalis (Navratri, Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas) announced a week ahead.
- **Occasions:** birthday and anniversary packages (decor + cake + thali), kitty
  parties, small functions — the site's group-enquiry form and "How many of
  you?" table setter already feed these to WhatsApp.
- **Loyalty:** a simple stamp card — 6th thali free (paper or WhatsApp).
- **WhatsApp Business:** catalogue = menu, quick replies, an opt-in broadcast
  list for specials (only people who asked to join).

## 5. Instagram — show the place, let guests show it too

- **Reels > posts.** 3 a week: thali being served, the bamboo room as the
  lamps come on, the kitchen, a family celebrating (with consent).
- **A photo spot in the restaurant** — a bamboo frame with "The Bamboo House ·
  Panna" — so guests post and tag you.
- Repost guests' stories; a monthly "guest photo of the month".
- Bio link → the website (Hindi link too). Handle: if it's not a resort, a
  clearer handle helps (e.g. @thebamboohouse.panna) — owner's call.

## 7. Measure what works (monthly)

- Google Business Profile: calls, direction requests, profile views, photo views.
- Reviews: count and average (target 200 / 4.6+ in six months).
- Website (GA4): call / WhatsApp / directions clicks, game shares.
- Instagram: followers, reel reach, tags.
- Ask at the counter: "How did you hear about us?" (tally sheet).

## 30 / 60 / 90 days

- **30:** claim + complete Google listing, photo shoot, QR table cards,
  reviews routine, WhatsApp Business, site launch (prototype mode off).
- **60:** weekly Google Posts + 3 reels a week, festival thali, occasions
  packages, driver partnerships, Tripadvisor / Zomato listings.
- **90:** game-at-the-table with monthly prize, first travel guides, review
  target check, adjust based on what the numbers say.
